About Me

Tong Li is a Full Professor in the College of Computer Science and Electronic Engineering at Hunan University. He previously served as a Research Assistant Professor in the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University and was a postdoctoral scholar there advised by Prof. Yong Li. He received dual PhDs: a PhD in Computer Science from the University of Helsinki (2022) and a PhD in Computer Science and Engineering from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (2021), co-advised by Prof. Pan Hui and Prof. Sasu Tarkoma. Dr. Li has authored over 80 publications in leading venues, including Nature Sustainability, KDD, The Web Conference (WWW), CoNEXT, UbiComp, Proceedings of the IEEE, IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing (TMC), IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering (TKDE), and ACM Transactions on Knowledge Discovery from Data (TKDD). He serves on the program committees of KDD, WWW, AAAI, IJCAI, and related venues. His research interests include digital twins for mobile networks, spatiotemporal data mining, large language models (LLM) and agents, and AI for healthcare.
